InfiniTime brings clocks, calendars and everyday planning tools together. Set alarms for your routine, keep notes and reminders, follow local times around the world and view dates in the Persian, Gregorian and Hijri calendars.

## Features

- **Clock and alarms:** Date and time scheduling, repeat rules, snooze options, sound, vibration and customizable alarm screens.
- **Calendars and notes:** Three calendars, occasions and holidays, notes, reminders and priority markers on calendar days.
- **Smart entry:** Create alarms and notes from typed or dictated text.
- **Timer and stopwatch:** Countdowns, target dates and times, lap recording and saved configurations.
- **World clocks:** Add cities, view their local dates and times and choose a time reference for alarms.
- **Prayer times and adhan:** Prayer schedules for a selected location, with notification and audio settings.
- **Compass and qibla:** Direction and device-level readings, subject to your phone’s available sensors.
- **Personalization:** Persian, English and Arabic, light and dark modes, widgets and a persistent daily date notification.

## Installation

**Requires Android 8.0 or later.**

1. Download `InfiniTime.apk` using the link at the top of this page.
2. Open it from Downloads or your file manager and confirm installation. If Android asks, allow installation for that file manager.
3. Open InfiniTime and configure alarm and notification permissions during setup.

Install a new release over your existing version to update. Builds with update checking can offer newer versions while the app is open; downloading and installing remain your choice.
